**rclone-vault-sync** is a personal tool used only by the owner of this site (treno87@gmail.com).

- It uses Google OAuth to access the owner's own Google Drive and syncs files between that Drive and the owner's own computer using [rclone](https://rclone.org/).
- It does not collect, store, or share any user data with third parties.
- Data accessed through the Google Drive API stays in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's computer.
- No other users are served by this app.
